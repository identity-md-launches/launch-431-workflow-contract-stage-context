The additional suite uses the real fixed-supply LaunchToken and constructor-created game contracts. It needs no network, forks, extra packages, environment writes, or configuration changes.

`PactsAdversarial.t.sol` adds failed-transfer and bond-lock rollback, revoked-majority execution, malformed proposals, packing overflow, one-unit amounts, exact epoch boundaries, full-supply purchases, and fee extremes. Three properties run 1,000 inputs each: packing round trips, fee conservation across rounding boundaries, and battle outcomes independent of declaration order. A snapshot comparison also checks chunked versus single-call settlement.

`PactsInvariant.t.sol` runs 256 random sequences of 64 handler calls across six actors and three guilds. It changes membership and votes, buys and reinforces troops, declares attacks, advances and settles epochs, funds and distributes treasuries, signs and settles pacts, attempts season closing in arbitrary order, claims prizes and honour banners, transfers banners directly and through approvals, and directly donates tokens. Seeded state and deterministic handler tests ensure custody, battles, bonds and actual prize/treasury payouts are exercised. All token funding comes from constructor supply; none of the contracts are given synthetic balances or privileged storage changes.

Its eight invariants check supply and the complete holder set, independently recorded cash flows and backing of assigned prizes, exact backing of all Realm liabilities, exact backing of Season claims and open pools, map counts and no troop creation, locked bonds against outstanding pacts, current membership and majority counts, and permanence of closed season results and prize pools. Realm accounting includes treasuries, pending income, locked bonds, uncredited epoch pools and the carried remainder, including between settlement chunks. Both custody equalities account separately for unsolicited donations and allow no dust tolerance. Handler postconditions additionally check payout amounts, repeat-claim rejection, expulsion bans, harvest idempotence, and settlement monotonicity. Eligible treasury distributions must succeed; unexpected handler reverts fail the campaign.

The four defects reported in the previous round have been repaired in the accepted implementation. This revision updates the existing handler and strengthens its properties to exercise those repairs:

- Out-of-order closing must revert with `PreviousSeasonNotClosed` without changing the closing cursor or pool, including while the preceding season is partially closed. Chronological catch-up remains possible, closed pools cannot receive late rollover, and every retained token is represented by a claim or an open pool.
- Income liabilities must equal the actual Realm balance after subtracting donations. A deterministic sequence checks the equality during partial battle settlement, after a treasury payout, and after completing the epoch; the existing Realm regressions cover idle-epoch rounding and changing tile ownership.
- Settlement during a betrayal epoch must revert with `PactStillActive` and preserve both bonds, treasuries and the pact. A deterministic handler sequence includes a same-epoch counterattack and then settles both betrayals at the next epoch boundary.
- Winning members with a zero token prize must still claim a victory banner exactly once. The handler uses `claimRank` for eligibility, verifies banner guild/rank metadata, and clears both claim fields. A deterministic sequence claims after the winner switches guilds and checks that the banner still identifies the winning guild.

The original adversarial tests and source contributor's repair regressions are retained. No source, configuration, dependency, ABI or manifest changes are part of this test revision.

Run `forge build` and `forge test` to check the deliverable. Fuzz and invariant counts are specified inline in the test files.
