The additional suite uses the real fixed-supply LaunchToken and constructor-created game contracts. It needs no network, forks, extra packages, environment writes, or configuration changes.

`PactsAdversarial.t.sol` adds failed-transfer and bond-lock rollback, revoked-majority execution, malformed proposals, packing overflow, one-unit amounts, exact epoch boundaries, full-supply purchases, and fee extremes. Three properties run 1,000 inputs each: packing round trips (attack, explicit-defender and reinforcement payloads), fee conservation across rounding boundaries, and battle outcomes independent of declaration order. A snapshot comparison also checks chunked versus single-call settlement.

`PactsInvariant.t.sol` runs 256 random sequences of 64 handler calls across six actors and three guilds. It changes membership and votes, buys and reinforces troops, declares attacks, advances and settles epochs, funds and distributes treasuries, signs and settles pacts, attempts season closing in arbitrary order, claims prizes and honour banners, transfers banners directly and through approvals, and directly donates tokens. Seeded state and deterministic handler tests ensure custody, battles, bonds and actual prize/treasury payouts are exercised. All token funding comes from constructor supply; none of the contracts are given synthetic balances or privileged storage changes.

Its eight invariants check supply and the complete holder set, independently recorded cash flows and backing of assigned prizes, exact backing of all Realm liabilities, exact backing of Season claims and open pools, map counts and no troop creation, locked bonds against outstanding pacts, current membership and majority counts, and permanence of closed season results and prize pools. Realm accounting includes treasuries, pending income, locked bonds, uncredited epoch pools and the carried remainder, including between settlement chunks. Both custody equalities account separately for unsolicited donations and allow no dust tolerance. Handler postconditions additionally check payout amounts, repeat-claim rejection, expulsion bans, harvest idempotence, and settlement monotonicity. Eligible treasury distributions must succeed; unexpected handler reverts fail the campaign.

This revision follows the source contributor's second revision, which changed the accepted interface: treasury purchases and reinforcements now execute voted proposals, attack payloads bind the expected defender, proposals expire after one day, pact bonds have a minimum of one troop's price, signing is refused while an attack between the partners is in flight in the signing epoch, and prize and honour eligibility require membership from before the season end. The handler and the adversarial tests were updated to that interface and now also exercise its failure paths:

- Treasury purchases and reinforcements in the handler go through passed `KIND_BUY_TROOPS` and `KIND_REINFORCE` votes, check the exact treasury charge and garrison change, and reject reuse of a consumed vote. Pact signing bounds bonds at the minimum, checks the bonds leave the treasuries, and, when the two guilds attacked each other in the current epoch, requires the `AttackInFlight` refusal to consume nothing. Honour claims by a member who joined at or after the season end must be refused with `JoinedAfterSeason`; successful claims are checked for metadata and single use. Deterministic sequences pin both refusal branches.
- Adversarial unit tests cover: every executor (treasury purchase, distribution, reinforcement, attack, expulsion, pact) refusing a proposal exactly one day old while the vote itself stays intact; wrong kind, unknown, unvoted, foreign-guild and former-member treasury purchases; zero, unaffordable and overflowing purchase payloads that leave the vote executable; reinforcement payloads for foreign, unheld or invalid tiles, zero or excessive counts and unsettled epochs; attack votes fired at a tile whose holder changed (including a vote naming a defender that never held the tile, and a holder that changed twice); bonds one unit below the minimum on either side; honour banners for members joining exactly at the season end, non-members and rejoiners; and prize shares that skip post-season joiners while the season contract stays exactly backed.

One defect could not be tested around and is reported in `.imd-findings.json` with a failing proof: an attack on the future partner declared in the previous epoch and still unsettled does not block signing and is then hidden by the pact snapshot, so the capture inside the pact window is not a betrayal. The adversarial and handler tests assert only the documented single-epoch refusal.

The original adversarial tests and source contributor's repair regressions are retained. No source, configuration, dependency, ABI or manifest changes are part of this test revision.

Run `forge build` and `forge test` to check the deliverable. Fuzz and invariant counts are specified inline in the test files.
